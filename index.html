THE CHILO METHOD

Substance
over noise.

Each professional conversation should start with documented information, not assumptions. We are building a system that puts provenance, independent laboratory results, and honest stock status at the centre.

01

Source with intention.

Supplier and farm details are reviewed before inclusion in verified buyer material.

02

Verify every batch.

COA, sample identity, batch data and specialist review are recorded when supplied and verified.

03

Communicate clearly.

Current availability and documents remain unconfirmed until the responsible team validates them.
