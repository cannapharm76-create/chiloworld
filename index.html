Medical-market first.

Thai cannabis rules have changed materially. Cannabis flower is regulated, and current requirements include restrictions on electronic sales and a medical-use framework for authorised establishments. Chilo Exotics therefore uses a direct B2B enquiry model rather than an online shop.
