# Photograph and batch verification checklist

For each historical strain-name record:

- [ ] Obtain a genuine image of the exact flower batch from Chilo or its farm, with reuse permission.
- [ ] Record the photographer and rights/permission.
- [ ] Capture the farm/company name, batch/lot ID and verified cultivation method.
- [ ] Add the COA from the laboratory and independently cross-check sample identity against the batch.
- [ ] Confirm THC/CBD and other technical fields only when the certificate actually states them.
- [ ] Confirm whether there is physically available stock, when it was last updated and who verified it.
- [ ] Check the site and any publication against current Thailand rules with qualified local legal counsel.
- [ ] Never treat client-side access controls or `robots.txt` as adequate security for a restricted catalog.
